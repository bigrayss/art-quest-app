# 学生题面中英对照 / Learner-facing wording

每个年龄段固定展示五道（含 17 岁及以上一段），选择一道完成。提示按需显示；任务属性与研究分析不显示给学生。

Five fixed choices are offered in each age band. Complete one. Hints are shown on request; research metadata is not learner-facing.

## B1_OB_01｜两片叶子 / Two Leaves

看看图里的两片叶子，把它们都画下来。让人能看出哪片宽、哪片长，再画上你看到的叶柄和叶脉。只画线条也可以。

Look at the two leaves in the reference and draw both. Show which one is broad and which one is long, and add the stems and veins you can see. A line drawing is fine.

**可选提示 / Optional hints**

- 先看叶子的外边，再看里面的线。 / Look at the outside edges first, then the lines inside.
- 可以把两片叶子画得大一些。 / You may draw the leaves fairly large.

**可跳过的完成后问题 / Skippable reflection**

你觉得这两片叶子哪里最不一样？

Where do you think the two leaves differ most?

Draw something you play with someone else, or invent a game. Show who is playing and what they are doing.

**可选提示 / Optional hints**

**可跳过的完成后问题 / Skippable reflection**

Turn the shoe in the reference into a creature that lives in a great forest. Show how it lives there, and keep some features of the shoe so that people can still tell it came from a shoe. You decide which parts to keep.

## B6_DS_01｜我的画廊标志 / A Sign for My Gallery

这个 app 里有一面挂着你作品的画廊。为它设计一个标志，让看到的人知道这是你的地方。图形和颜色由你决定。

This app has a gallery wall that shows your drawings. Design a sign for it so that people know this is your place. Choose the shapes and colours yourself.

- 先想一个最能代表你的东西。 / Think of the one thing that best stands for you.
- 标志缩小了也要认得出。 / The sign should still be recognisable when small.

别人看到这个标志会想到你的什么？
