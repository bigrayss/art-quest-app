彩绘冒险（KidsArtQuest）是一个美术教育研究项目中使用的儿童绘画应用。孩子在应用里完成绘画任务，得到一段不打分的反馈，再回去改一改。研究团队借此了解儿童绘画与修改的过程。

本政策说明我们处理哪些个人信息、为什么处理、谁能看到、保存多久，以及孩子和监护人如何行使权利。请监护人在同意孩子参加研究前通读本政策；我们也会用孩子能听懂的话向孩子本人说明一遍。

1适用范围

本政策适用于彩绘冒险的网页版（art.ddhulu.cn）与 iPadOS 版应用，以及研究团队与参与研究的美术教师在研究过程中对上述应用所产生数据的处理。

本政策不适用于：学校自行收集并保管的名单、同意书与课堂记录；孩子或教师在本应用之外使用的其他软件与服务。

研究组联系邮箱：mingrui.ye@kcl.ac.uk（数据相关请求、查阅、删除、撤回同意均可发至此邮箱）。

本研究已通过伦理审查：华东师范大学 [伦理审批编号：待填]；伦敦国王学院 [Ethics reference：待填]。

4我们不处理的信息

我们不收集、不要求、不推断：真实姓名、身份证号、学号、学校与班级、家庭住址、电话号码、监护人联系方式（同意书由学校保管，不进入应用）、精确地理位置、通讯录、相册与摄像头内容、人脸或其他生物识别信息、健康信息、设备唯一标识符用于广告追踪、其他应用的使用数据。

孩子可以把作品保存到自己设备的相册；该操作在设备本地完成，我们不会读取相册。

5处理目的与法律依据

处理目的

  1. 研究儿童如何绘画、如何理解反馈，以及收到反馈后画面发生了什么变化。
  2. 改进应用的任务设计、反馈措辞与界面。
  3. 训练与评估自动评价儿童绘画的模型。用于此目的的作品会先经人工逐张检查，含可识别信息（例如孩子把自己的名字写进画面）的不予使用。
  4. 以论文、会议报告与课程材料的形式发表研究结果。发表的是统计结果与经去标识化的示例，不含孩子的真实身份。

法律依据

  • 中国内地：依据《中华人民共和国个人信息保护法》第十三条第一款第一项（取得个人同意），并按第三十一条就未满十四周岁未成年人的个人信息取得其父母或其他监护人的同意。
  • 英国：依据 UK GDPR 第六条第一款第(e)项（为公共利益执行任务——高等教育机构开展的学术研究）；如处理涉及特殊类别个人数据，依据第九条第二款第(j)项（科学研究目的）结合 Data Protection Act 2018 Schedule 1 Part 1。

撤回同意不影响撤回之前已经进行的处理的合法性，但撤回之后我们将停止处理，并按第 11 节删除数据。

6AI 反馈与数据跨境传输

孩子画完后，应用会生成一段不打分的反馈；创作过程中的「彩点」陪伴也由同一服务生成。为此，以下内容会发送给境外 AI 服务提供方 Anthropic PBC（美国）的 Claude API：

  • 画面图像（成品图；比较修改前后时会同时发送两张）；
  • 任务标题与任务说明文字；
  • 孩子画前选择的心情与写下的那句「想画什么」；
  • 孩子画后的三道自评答案；
  • 在为孩子挑选下一个任务时，还包括该孩子过往创作的摘要（任务编号、时长、是否修改过、自评与各维度评价）。

不会发送：昵称、账号编号、暗号、年龄、设备信息、教师评语原文。

关于该传输：服务提供方按其商用条款仅为生成本次回复而处理上述内容，不将其用于训练模型；传输使用 HTTPS 加密。[跨境传输的法律机制与所依据的影响评估结论：待填（中国内地侧的出境路径／英国侧的 IDTA 或等效安排）]

如果研究员未为服务器配置该 AI 服务，应用会改用预先写好的本地模板生成反馈；此时作品与上述内容不会离开我们的服务器。应用内「我的」页面会显示当前使用的是哪一种。孩子的作品不会被发送给任何其他第三方服务。

8存储地点与保留期限

数据存放在位于中国大陆的服务器（腾讯云，上海地域），应用与服务器之间全程使用 HTTPS 加密传输。

  • 原始数据（作品、过程记录、自评、日志、教师评分）：在研究结束后保留 5 年，以便同行复核；期满后删除或彻底去标识化。
  • 账号信息：在研究结束时删除；昵称在研究数据中以随机编号替代。
  • 已退出或要求删除的数据：按第 11 节处理，自收到请求起 30 日内删除。
  • 已发表的统计结果与经同意公开的数据集：无法撤回，其中不含可识别孩子身份的信息。

9安全措施

  • 暗号以 PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 加盐哈希存储，不保存原文，忘记暗号只能重设、无法找回。
  • 应用与服务器之间全程 HTTPS；服务器仅开放必要端口。
  • 研究员接口与教师接口各自需要独立令牌；教师需邀请码注册；服务器不提供任何可列出全部数据的公开接口。
  • 作品与过程记录按会话分目录存放，访问受账号归属校验限制：一个账号只能读写属于自己的会话。
  • 如发生可能危害个人权益的个人信息泄露，我们将按法律要求通知监管机构，并通过学校通知监护人。

10对未成年人的专门规则

本应用的使用者是 6 至 14 岁儿童。依据《个人信息保护法》第三十一条，我们制定本节作为处理未满十四周岁未成年人个人信息的专门规则：

  • 参加研究前，由学校与研究团队向监护人说明并取得书面同意；未取得同意的孩子不会被纳入研究，也不会在应用内创建账号。
  • 在孩子第一次使用前，用孩子能听懂的话向孩子本人说明一遍：会记录什么、谁会看到、可以不做、可以随时停下。
  • 应用不要求孩子填写任何真实身份信息；昵称栏的提示语明确建议使用代号。
  • 收集的信息限于研究与应用运行所必需的最小范围；不对孩子做画像、不做个性化广告推送。
  • 作品是否出现在「大家的画廊」由孩子本人逐幅决定；作品是否进入公开数据集由监护人单独决定。两者缺一不可。
  • 孩子在应用内可以自己删除自己的作品，无需经过研究团队。

KidsArtQuest (彩绘冒险) is a children's drawing app used in an art-education research project. Children complete drawing tasks, receive feedback that carries no score, and go back to revise. The research team uses what happens in between to study how children draw and revise.

This policy explains what personal information we process, why, who can see it, how long we keep it, and how children and their guardians can exercise their rights. Guardians should read it in full before consenting; we also explain it to each child in words they can understand.

1Scope

This policy covers the KidsArtQuest web app (art.ddhulu.cn) and the iPadOS app, and the processing by the research team and participating art teachers of the data those apps produce.

It does not cover registers, consent forms or classroom records that a school collects and keeps itself, nor any other software a child or teacher uses outside this app.

4What we do not process

We do not collect, request or infer: real names, national ID or student numbers, school or class, home address, phone numbers, guardian contact details (consent forms stay with the school and never enter the app), precise location, contacts, photo library or camera content, faces or other biometric data, health data, advertising identifiers, or usage data from other apps.

A child can save a drawing to their own device's photo library; that happens locally on the device and we never read the library.

5Purposes and legal bases

Purposes

  1. To study how children draw, how they understand feedback, and what changes in the picture afterwards.
  2. To improve the app's tasks, the wording of its feedback, and its interface.
  3. To train and evaluate models that assess children's drawings. Every drawing used for this is checked by a person first; any drawing containing identifying information (for example a child who wrote their own name on it) is excluded.
  4. To publish findings in papers, conference reports and teaching material. What is published is statistics and de-identified examples, never a child's identity.

Legal bases

  • Mainland China: consent under Article 13(1)(1) of the Personal Information Protection Law (PIPL), together with the consent of a parent or other guardian for children under 14 under Article 31.
  • United Kingdom: UK GDPR Article 6(1)(e) — a task carried out in the public interest, namely academic research by higher-education institutions. Where special category data is involved, Article 9(2)(j) (scientific research) with Schedule 1 Part 1 of the Data Protection Act 2018.

Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal; after it, we stop processing and delete data as described in section 11.

6AI feedback and transfers abroad

After a child finishes a drawing, the app generates feedback that carries no score; the in-canvas companion uses the same service. For this, the following is sent to Anthropic PBC (United States) via the Claude API:

  • the drawing (and both images when before and after are compared);
  • the task title and task text;
  • the mood the child chose and the one line they wrote about what they wanted to draw;
  • the child's three short self-report answers;
  • when choosing the child's next task, a digest of their earlier sessions (task ids, duration, whether revised, self-reports and dimension notes).

Never sent: nickname, account id, PIN, age, device information, or teachers' written comments.

8Storage and retention

Data is held on servers in mainland China (Tencent Cloud, Shanghai region). Traffic between the app and the server is encrypted with HTTPS throughout.

  • Raw data (drawings, process records, self-reports, logs, teacher ratings): kept for 5 years after the study ends so that findings can be checked, then deleted or fully de-identified.
  • Account data: deleted when the study ends; nicknames are replaced by random identifiers in the research data.
  • Withdrawn or deletion-requested data: handled under section 11 and deleted within 30 days of the request.
  • Published statistics and consented public datasets: cannot be recalled, and contain nothing that identifies a child.

9Security

  • PINs are stored salted and hashed (PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256); the original is never stored, so a forgotten PIN can be reset but not retrieved.
  • HTTPS between app and server; only the necessary ports are open.
  • Researcher and teacher endpoints each require their own token, and teachers register with an invitation code. No public endpoint can list all data.
  • Drawings and process records are stored per session and access is checked against account ownership: an account can read and write only its own sessions.
  • If a breach occurs that could harm someone's rights, we will notify the regulator as required by law and notify guardians through the school.

10Specific rules for children

Our users are children aged 6–14. As required by PIPL Article 31 for children under 14, these are our specific rules:

  • Before taking part, the school and research team explain the study to the guardian and obtain written consent. A child without consent is not enrolled and no account is created.
  • Before a child's first use, we explain to the child, in words they can follow: what is recorded, who sees it, that they may decline, and that they may stop at any time.
  • The app never asks for real identity information; the nickname field tells children a code name is fine.
  • We collect only what the research and the app need; we build no profiles for advertising and send no personalised ads.
  • Whether a drawing appears in the shared gallery is decided by the child, drawing by drawing. Whether it enters a public dataset is decided separately by a guardian. Both are required.
  • A child can delete their own drawings inside the app without going through the research team.
