彩绘冒险（KidsArtQuest）是一款用于美术教育研究的儿童绘画应用，面向 6 岁及以上的美术初学者、儿童和青少年。用户可以在应用中完成绘画任务、获得不计分的反馈，并根据反馈继续修改作品。研究团队通过相关数据研究儿童与青少年的绘画、反馈理解和修改过程。

本政策说明我们处理哪些个人信息、为什么处理、谁可以接触这些信息、数据如何保存，以及用户和监护人可以如何行使相关权利。对于未成年用户，请监护人在同意其参加研究前阅读本政策。研究开始前，我们也会使用适合儿童理解的方式向其说明相关内容。

1适用范围

本政策适用于彩绘冒险 KidsArtQuest 的网页版与 iPadOS 版，以及研究团队和参与研究的美术教师在使用上述应用过程中进行的相关数据处理活动。

本政策不适用于学校自行收集和保管的学生名单、监护人同意书、课堂记录，以及用户、教师或监护人在 KidsArtQuest 之外使用的其他软件和服务。

如学校需要保存参与者真实身份与研究代号之间的对应关系，该信息原则上由学校或指定研究人员通过独立方式保管，不进入 KidsArtQuest 普通研究数据库。

2我们是谁

本研究由华东师范大学与伦敦国王学院相关研究团队共同开展。

两校在本研究中的具体个人信息处理责任，将根据研究协议、数据处理安排和适用的数据保护要求确定。

研究团队联系邮箱：

mingrui.ye@kcl.ac.uk

您可以通过该邮箱联系我们处理数据查阅、更正、删除、退出研究以及其他与隐私和个人信息有关的请求。

3我们处理哪些个人信息

为开展研究和保证应用正常运行，我们可能处理以下信息：

信息类别具体内容处理目的
账号信息用户自行设置的昵称或研究代号、四位数字暗号、可选年龄、账号创建和登录时间。暗号以加盐哈希方式保存，不保存明文支持登录和作品归属；关联同一参与者的多次创作
绘画作品修改前和修改后的作品图像及相关作品文件研究分析、教师评分、生成反馈、模型训练与评估
绘画过程笔触轨迹、颜色、笔刷、时间戳、撤销与重做，以及按照研究设置生成的过程画面分析作画顺序、停顿、修改和工具使用行为
自述与自评画前心情、“想画什么”、任务难度、自我评价、愉悦度、“哪里最难”等研究创作意图、自我评价及其与作品表现之间的关系
操作日志点击与页面停留、工具使用、是否查看参考图、是否打开提示、界面语言、界面模式和相关时间戳研究应用使用和反馈阅读行为；改进界面和排查故障
教师评分参与研究的美术教师对作品进行的维度评分、文字评语和评分时间建立专家评价基准；训练和评估自动评价模型
设备信息屏幕尺寸、像素比、浏览器或操作系统版本、界面语言、时区、应用版本确保应用正常运行并分析设备差异

绘画过程中，部分研究任务可能按照固定时间间隔保存过程画面，用于分析绘画顺序、停顿和修改行为。具体记录方式会根据研究任务设置进行。

用户填写的部分自述或自评内容可以根据界面提示选择跳过。

我们不使用用于商业广告或跨站追踪的 Cookie。应用仅使用维持登录状态、保存当前语言及保证基本运行所必要的 Cookie 或本地存储。

4我们不处理的信息

KidsArtQuest 不要求用户提供真实姓名、身份证号码、学号、学校名称、班级、家庭住址、电话号码、监护人联系方式、精确地理位置、通讯录、相册中的其他照片、摄像头内容、麦克风录音、人脸或其他生物识别信息。

我们不会为了广告追踪收集设备唯一标识符，也不会读取其他应用的使用情况。

监护人联系方式、真实身份信息或研究同意书如由学校或研究团队另行收集，将按照独立的研究管理流程保存，不进入 KidsArtQuest 普通账号信息。

用户可以将自己的作品保存到设备本地。该功能不会因此授权我们读取设备相册中已有的其他照片或内容。

5处理目的与法律依据

我们处理上述信息，主要用于研究儿童和青少年如何绘画、如何理解反馈，以及收到反馈后作品和绘画过程发生的变化；改进 KidsArtQuest 的绘画任务、反馈内容、界面和研究设计；训练和评估儿童绘画自动评价、过程建模及反馈生成模型；以及进行学术论文、会议报告、研究报告和其他研究成果分析。

用于模型研究、学术展示或其他研究输出前，我们会尽可能减少数据与参与者真实身份之间的关联。如果作品中出现真实姓名、学校名称、电话号码等明显身份信息，将根据研究方案进行排除、遮挡或其他适当处理。

在中国大陆开展的数据处理，将依据《中华人民共和国个人信息保护法》及其他适用法律进行。对于以同意为基础的个人信息处理，我们会依法取得相应同意；对于不满十四周岁未成年人的个人信息，将取得其父母或者其他监护人的同意，并采取适用于未成年人个人信息的专门保护措施。

对于伦敦国王学院负责的数据处理，其学术研究活动通常依据 UK GDPR 下有关公共任务的法律基础开展。研究过程中如涉及特殊类别个人数据，将仅在适用法律和相关研究条件允许的范围内进行处理。

监护人同意未成年人参加研究，与数据保护法律意义上的个人信息处理法律依据并不完全相同。参与者或监护人可以随时决定停止继续参加研究。

6AI 反馈与数据跨境传输

KidsArtQuest 可能使用外部 AI 服务生成不计分的绘画反馈，以及研究所需的提示或个性化反馈。目前使用的相关服务可能包括 Anthropic PBC 提供的 Claude API。

在启用相关 AI 功能时，系统可能发送当前作品图像、修改前后的作品、任务标题与任务说明、用户填写的“想画什么”、与当前任务有关的简单自评，以及生成个性化反馈所必需的历史创作摘要。

历史创作摘要可能包括任务编号、绘画时长、是否进行修改、自评以及经处理后的评价结果。所有发送内容将限制在完成相应功能所需要的范围内。

外部 AI 请求原则上不会包含用户昵称、登录暗号、KidsArtQuest 账号凭证、学校与班级、监护人联系方式、设备信息以及教师完整评语原文。

相关数据通过加密连接传输。

研究团队将根据实际使用的 API 商业条款、数据处理协议和服务配置确定 AI 服务提供方的数据处理范围。研究项目不会授权 AI 服务提供方将参与者数据用于广告或商业画像。

如相关个人信息涉及跨境传输，我们将根据适用的数据保护法律完成必要的告知、授权以及其他数据保护安排。

如果研究服务器没有启用外部 AI 服务，应用可以使用本地规则、预先准备的反馈模板或关闭相应 AI 功能。

7信息的共享与公开

我们仅在研究和应用正常运行所需要的范围内共享相关信息。

经授权的研究人员可以根据各自职责访问研究数据。参与研究的美术教师仅可查看完成评分所需要的作品、过程画面和研究昵称或代号，不会获得登录暗号或与评分无关的信息。

在启用 AI 功能时，相关服务提供方只会收到完成相应功能所必要的数据。提供服务器、存储等技术服务的服务商仅在提供相关服务所需要的范围内处理数据。

如果用户选择将作品展示在应用内画廊中，其他用户可以看到被公开的作品及相应展示昵称。未经选择公开的作品不会因此向其他用户展示。

如未来建立公开研究数据集，仅会在取得相应授权并完成去标识化或匿名化处理后使用有关数据。

除上述情况、法律法规另有规定或者有权机关依法提出要求外，我们不会出售、交换或向第三方提供参与者个人信息用于商业广告和营销。

8作品展示与公开研究数据集

参加研究不等于同意公开作品，也不等于同意作品进入公开研究数据集。

KidsArtQuest 可能提供“大家的画廊”等应用内作品展示功能。只有符合相应展示条件并按照应用设置选择公开的作品，才会出现在相关区域。

用户可以按照应用提供的功能撤回作品展示。撤回后，该作品将不再继续显示在相应在线展示区域。

如研究团队未来计划将部分作品或绘画过程记录纳入公开研究数据集，将进行相应的去标识化或匿名化处理，并在监护人或参与者同意流程中单独征求授权。

不同意作品展示或公开研究数据，不影响参与其他研究活动。

已经完成不可逆匿名化的数据、已经发表的汇总研究结果，或者已经合法公开且无法合理重新识别个人的数据，通常无法再针对某一名参与者单独撤回。

9数据存储与保留

KidsArtQuest 的研究数据存储于受访问控制保护的研究服务器中。应用与服务器之间使用 HTTPS 等加密方式传输数据。

研究数据可能包括绘画作品、绘画过程、自评、操作日志、教师评分及相关研究标注。

我们仅在完成研究、研究复核、论文发表、学术诚信以及适用的数据管理要求所必要的期限内保存相关数据。

保存期限届满后，相关数据将根据研究和数据管理要求删除，或者进行无法合理重新识别个人的匿名化处理。

账号登录信息在不再需要参与者登录后，将根据研究需要进行删除或去标识化。研究昵称可以由随机研究编号替代。

因参与者退出研究或提出删除请求而需要处理的数据，将按照本政策有关权利和退出研究的规定处理。

已经纳入正常技术备份的数据可能需要在备份周期结束后完成清除。在此期间，该数据不会重新用于新的研究分析。

10安全措施

我们采取与研究数据风险相适应的技术和管理措施保护数据安全。

登录暗号不会以明文方式保存，而是通过加盐哈希等方式进行保护。应用和服务器之间使用加密连接。

研究人员和参与研究的教师使用独立的账号、令牌或其他授权方式访问系统。不同角色按照研究需要获得不同的数据访问权限，并遵循最小权限原则。

研究账号与真实身份信息尽可能分离。研究数据按照参与者、作品或研究会话进行管理，并设置必要的访问控制。

教师账号通过授权方式创建。服务器不会提供任何无需身份验证即可公开列出全部参与者研究数据的接口。

研究人员和参与研究的教师需要遵守相应的研究保密和数据保护要求。

如果发生可能对参与者个人权益造成风险的数据泄露或其他安全事件，我们将采取措施控制和补救风险，并根据适用法律判断是否需要向有关机构以及受影响的参与者或监护人进行通知。

11对未成年人的专门规则

KidsArtQuest 面向 6 岁及以上的美术初学者、儿童和青少年。对于未成年参与者，我们会采取与其年龄和研究安排相适应的保护措施。

在需要监护人同意的情况下，参与研究前会取得监护人的相应同意，并使用适合儿童理解的方式向参与者说明会记录哪些信息、为什么记录、谁可能看到，以及如何停止继续参加。

即使监护人已经同意，未成年参与者本人仍然可以表示不愿参加，也可以在研究过程中停止继续参与。

应用不要求用户填写真实姓名、学号、学校或班级等直接身份信息，并会提示使用昵称或研究代号。

收集的信息限于研究和应用正常运行所需要的范围。未成年人的研究数据不会用于个性化商业广告、跨应用广告追踪或其他商业营销。

AI 反馈和自动评价不会单独用于决定参与者的学校成绩、升学、奖励、处罚或其他重要教育权益。

参加研究、将作品展示在应用内画廊以及允许数据进入公开研究数据集属于不同事项，不会因为参加研究而自动同意公开作品或数据。

对于不满十四周岁的儿童，我们将按照中国《中华人民共和国个人信息保护法》及其他适用的未成年人个人信息保护规定，取得监护人的相应同意并采取专门保护措施。

12您和孩子的权利

参与者和监护人可以根据适用法律及研究安排，对相关数据提出查阅、更正、复制、删除、限制处理、反对处理或撤回相关授权等请求。

参与者或监护人可以随时通知教师或研究团队停止继续参加研究，无需说明理由，也不会影响参与者在学校的学习、成绩或其他正常权益。停止参加后，我们将不再为该参与者收集新的研究数据。

停止参加研究与删除此前已经收集的数据并不完全相同。监护人或参与者可以另外提出数据删除请求。对于仍然可以与参与者关联的数据，我们会根据适用法律、研究要求以及数据是否已经匿名化，确定可以删除的范围。

如果 KidsArtQuest 当前版本提供自助删除功能，用户可以按照应用中的提示删除自己的作品。删除可能同时影响与该作品直接关联的笔触记录、过程画面、评分或其他研究数据，具体范围将在执行删除前通过应用界面说明。

参与者或监护人也可以通过研究邮箱申请查阅相关数据，或申请获得数据副本。在合理可行并符合法律规定的情况下，我们会使用常见的电子格式提供。

如果昵称、年龄或其他可更正的信息存在错误，可以联系我们进行更正。对于反映真实研究过程的历史笔触、原始作品和操作记录，我们通常不会通过“更正”改变已经发生的研究事实，但可以在适当情况下增加说明。

参与者或监护人可以分别撤回后续研究参与、作品展示、公开研究数据集以及其他依法需要单独授权的处理。撤回某一项可选授权并不必然影响继续参加其他研究活动。

在 UK GDPR 适用的范围内，参与者可能依法享有访问、更正、限制处理、反对处理以及其他有关权利。用于科学研究的数据处理在特定情况下可能依法适用相应例外或限制。

为了避免误删或误提供其他参与者的数据，我们可能需要通过研究昵称、研究代号、学校研究联系人或其他适当方式确认请求人与参与者之间的关系。

我们会按照适用法律在合理期限内处理有关请求。

13退出研究与既有数据

参与者或监护人可以随时决定不再继续参加研究。

退出后，我们将停止继续收集新的研究数据。

对于退出之前已经收集的数据，将根据研究参与说明、适用法律、数据是否已经匿名化以及保持研究完整性的需要，确定继续保留、停止进一步使用或删除的范围。

对于以同意作为法律基础的数据处理，撤回同意不会影响撤回之前已经依法完成的数据处理活动。

如果部分数据已经完成不可逆匿名化、已经纳入无法回溯到个人的汇总分析，或者已经依法发表，可能无法再针对单个参与者进行删除。

15本政策的变更

我们可能根据研究进展、应用功能、技术方案、法律要求或数据处理安排更新本政策。

更新后，我们会修改政策顶部的版本号和最后更新日期。

如果变化可能显著影响参与者的个人信息权益，例如新增重要个人信息类型、改变主要研究用途、新增重要的数据接收方、扩大数据公开范围或显著改变 AI 数据使用方式，我们将按照适用法律采取相应措施。

根据具体情况，我们可能通过应用、学校或其他研究沟通渠道通知参与者或监护人，并在必要时重新取得相应授权或同意。

KidsArtQuest (彩绘冒险) is a children's drawing application used for art-education research, intended for beginners in art, children and young people aged 6 and over. Users complete drawing tasks in the application, receive feedback that carries no score, and revise their work in light of that feedback. The research team uses the resulting data to study how children and young people draw, understand feedback and revise.

This policy explains what personal information we process, why we process it, who can access it, how the data is kept, and how users and guardians may exercise their rights. Where the user is a minor, guardians should read this policy before consenting to participation. Before the research begins, we also explain the relevant points to the user in a way suited to a child's understanding.

1Scope

This policy applies to the web and iPadOS versions of KidsArtQuest, and to the related data processing carried out by the research team and by participating art teachers in the course of using those applications.

It does not apply to student registers, guardian consent forms or classroom records that a school collects and keeps itself, nor to other software and services used by users, teachers or guardians outside KidsArtQuest.

Where a school needs to keep a record linking a participant's real identity to a study code, that information is in principle held separately by the school or a designated researcher and does not enter the ordinary KidsArtQuest research database.

4Information we do not process

KidsArtQuest does not ask users to provide real names, national ID numbers, student numbers, school names, class, home address, telephone numbers, guardian contact details, precise location, contacts, other photographs in the device's photo library, camera content, microphone recordings, faces or other biometric information.

We do not collect unique device identifiers for advertising tracking, and we do not read usage data from other applications.

Where guardian contact details, real identity information or research consent forms are collected separately by a school or by the research team, they are kept under a separate research management process and do not enter ordinary KidsArtQuest account information.

Users may save their own artwork to their device. Doing so does not authorise us to read other photographs or content already in the device's photo library.

5Purposes and legal bases

We process the information above mainly in order to study how children and young people draw, how they understand feedback, and what changes in the artwork and in the drawing process after feedback is received; to improve the drawing tasks, feedback content, interface and research design of KidsArtQuest; to train and evaluate models for automatic assessment of children's drawings, for process modelling and for feedback generation; and to carry out analysis for academic papers, conference presentations, research reports and other research outputs.

Before data is used for model research, academic presentation or other research outputs, we reduce as far as possible the link between the data and a participant's real identity. Where artwork contains evident identifying information such as a real name, school name or telephone number, it is excluded, masked or otherwise handled appropriately in accordance with the research protocol.

Processing carried out in mainland China is conducted in accordance with the Personal Information Protection Law of the People's Republic of China and other applicable law. Where processing is based on consent, we obtain that consent as required by law; for the personal information of minors under the age of fourteen, we obtain the consent of a parent or other guardian and apply the specific protective measures that apply to minors' personal information.

For processing for which King's College London is responsible, academic research activity is generally conducted on the legal basis relating to a public task under the UK GDPR. Where special category personal data is involved in the research, it is processed only to the extent permitted by applicable law and the relevant research conditions.

A guardian's consent for a minor to take part in research is not the same thing as the legal basis for processing personal information under data protection law. A participant or guardian may decide at any time to stop taking part.

6AI feedback and cross-border transfers

KidsArtQuest may use an external AI service to generate drawing feedback that carries no score, and the prompts or personalised feedback needed for the research. The services currently used may include the Claude API provided by Anthropic PBC.

When these AI functions are enabled, the system may send the current artwork image, the work before and after revision, the task title and task description, the "what I want to draw" text entered by the user, the brief self-assessment relating to the current task, and the summary of earlier work necessary to generate personalised feedback.

That summary of earlier work may include task identifiers, drawing duration, whether the work was revised, self-assessment, and processed assessment results. Everything sent is limited to what is needed to perform the corresponding function.

In principle, requests to the external AI service do not contain the user's nickname, sign-in PIN, KidsArtQuest account credentials, school or class, guardian contact details, device information, or the full original text of teachers' comments.

The data is transmitted over an encrypted connection.

The research team determines the scope of the AI service provider's processing on the basis of the commercial terms of the API actually used, the data processing agreement and the service configuration. The research project does not authorise the AI service provider to use participant data for advertising or commercial profiling.

Where personal information is transferred across borders, we complete the notification, authorisation and other data protection arrangements required under applicable data protection law.

If the research server does not have the external AI service enabled, the application can use local rules, prepared feedback templates, or switch the corresponding AI function off.

7Sharing and disclosure

We share information only to the extent needed for the research and for the normal operation of the application.

Authorised researchers may access research data according to their respective roles. Participating art teachers can see only the artwork, process images and study nickname or code needed to complete an assessment; they do not receive sign-in PINs or information unrelated to assessment.

When AI functions are enabled, the service provider receives only the data necessary to perform the corresponding function. Providers of technical services such as servers and storage process data only to the extent needed to provide those services.

If a user chooses to display a piece of work in the in-app gallery, other users can see that work and the nickname shown with it. Work that has not been chosen for display is not shown to other users.

If a public research dataset is established in future, the data concerned will be used only after the appropriate authorisation has been obtained and de-identification or anonymisation has been carried out.

Apart from the situations above, where laws or regulations provide otherwise, or where a competent authority makes a lawful request, we do not sell, exchange or provide participants' personal information to third parties for commercial advertising or marketing.

8Display of artwork and public research datasets

Taking part in the research does not mean consenting to the publication of artwork, nor to artwork entering a public research dataset.

KidsArtQuest may provide in-app display features such as the shared gallery. Only work that meets the applicable display conditions and has been chosen for display in accordance with the application's settings appears in those areas.

Users may withdraw a work from display using the functions the application provides. After withdrawal, the work is no longer shown in the corresponding online display area.

If the research team plans in future to include some artwork or drawing process records in a public research dataset, the corresponding de-identification or anonymisation will be carried out and separate authorisation will be sought through the guardian or participant consent process.

Declining display of artwork or inclusion in public research data does not affect participation in other research activities.

Data that has already been irreversibly anonymised, aggregate research results that have already been published, and data that has already been lawfully made public and cannot reasonably be re-identified, generally cannot be withdrawn for an individual participant.

9Storage and retention

KidsArtQuest research data is stored on research servers protected by access controls. Data is transmitted between the application and the server using encryption such as HTTPS.

Research data may include artwork, the drawing process, self-assessment, activity logs, teacher assessment and related research annotations.

We keep the data only for as long as is necessary to complete the research, to allow research review, publication and academic integrity requirements to be met, and to satisfy applicable data management requirements.

Once the retention period ends, the data is deleted in accordance with research and data management requirements, or anonymised so that an individual cannot reasonably be re-identified.

Sign-in information is deleted or de-identified according to the needs of the research once participants no longer need to sign in. Study nicknames may be replaced by random study identifiers.

Data that has to be handled because a participant withdraws or makes a deletion request is dealt with under the provisions of this policy on rights and on withdrawal.

Data already included in routine technical backups may only be cleared once the backup cycle ends. During that period, it is not reused for new research analysis.

10Security measures

We apply technical and organisational measures proportionate to the risks of the research data.

Sign-in PINs are not stored in plaintext; they are protected using a salted hash or similar method. Encrypted connections are used between the application and the server.

Researchers and participating teachers access the system using separate accounts, tokens or other forms of authorisation. Different roles receive different data access rights according to the needs of the research, following the principle of least privilege.

Research accounts are kept separate from real identity information as far as possible. Research data is managed by participant, artwork or research session, with the necessary access controls in place.

Teacher accounts are created through an authorisation process. The server provides no interface that lists all participants' research data publicly without authentication.

Researchers and participating teachers are required to comply with the applicable research confidentiality and data protection requirements.

If a data breach or other security incident occurs that may create a risk to participants' rights and interests, we take measures to contain and remedy the risk and determine, in accordance with applicable law, whether notification to the relevant authorities and to affected participants or guardians is required.

11Specific rules for minors

KidsArtQuest is intended for beginners in art, children and young people aged 6 and over. For participants who are minors, we apply protective measures appropriate to their age and to the research arrangements.

Where guardian consent is required, it is obtained before participation, and the participant is told, in a way suited to a child's understanding, what information will be recorded, why it is recorded, who may see it, and how to stop taking part.

Even where a guardian has consented, a participant who is a minor may still say that they do not wish to take part, and may stop taking part during the research.

The application does not ask users to enter direct identifying information such as a real name, student number, school or class, and it suggests using a nickname or study code.

The information collected is limited to what the research and the normal operation of the application require. Minors' research data is not used for personalised commercial advertising, cross-app advertising tracking or other commercial marketing.

AI feedback and automatic assessment are not used on their own to determine a participant's school marks, progression, rewards, penalties or other significant educational interests.

Taking part in the research, displaying artwork in the in-app gallery, and allowing data to enter a public research dataset are separate matters; taking part in the research does not automatically mean consenting to publication of artwork or data.

For children under the age of fourteen, we obtain the corresponding guardian consent and apply specific protective measures in accordance with the Personal Information Protection Law of the People's Republic of China and other applicable rules on the protection of minors' personal information.

12Your and your child's rights

Participants and guardians may, in accordance with applicable law and the research arrangements, request access to, correction of, a copy of, or deletion of the data, restriction of or objection to processing, or withdrawal of the relevant authorisation.

A participant or guardian may at any time tell a teacher or the research team that they are stopping participation. No reason need be given, and it has no effect on the participant's studies, marks or other ordinary interests at school. After participation stops, we collect no new research data for that participant.

Stopping participation is not the same as deleting data already collected. A guardian or participant may make a separate deletion request. For data that can still be linked to a participant, we determine the scope of what can be deleted on the basis of applicable law, research requirements, and whether the data has already been anonymised.

If the current version of KidsArtQuest provides a self-service deletion function, users may delete their own artwork by following the prompts in the application. Deletion may also affect stroke records, process images, assessments or other research data directly linked to that work; the scope is explained in the interface before the deletion is carried out.

A participant or guardian may also ask, by email to the research address, to see the relevant data or to obtain a copy of it. Where this is reasonably practicable and consistent with the law, we provide it in a commonly used electronic format.

If a nickname, age or other correctable item is wrong, you may contact us to have it corrected. For historical strokes, original artwork and activity records that reflect what actually happened during the research, we do not ordinarily use "correction" to change the research facts that have occurred, though a note may be added where appropriate.

A participant or guardian may separately withdraw from further participation, from display of artwork, from inclusion in public research datasets, and from other processing for which separate authorisation is required by law. Withdrawing one optional authorisation does not necessarily affect continued participation in other research activities.

To the extent that the UK GDPR applies, participants may have rights of access, correction, restriction of processing, objection to processing and other related rights. Processing for scientific research purposes may, in certain circumstances, be subject to exemptions or limitations provided by law.

To avoid wrongly deleting or disclosing another participant's data, we may need to confirm the relationship between the person making the request and the participant, through the study nickname, study code, the school's research contact or another appropriate means.

We handle such requests within a reasonable period in accordance with applicable law.

13Withdrawal and existing data

A participant or guardian may decide at any time to stop taking part in the research.

After withdrawal, we stop collecting new research data.

For data collected before the withdrawal, the scope of what is retained, no longer used, or deleted is determined on the basis of the participant information, applicable law, whether the data has already been anonymised, and the need to preserve the integrity of the research.

Where processing is based on consent, withdrawing consent does not affect processing lawfully carried out before the withdrawal.

Where data has already been irreversibly anonymised, incorporated into aggregate analysis that cannot be traced back to an individual, or lawfully published, deletion for an individual participant may no longer be possible.
